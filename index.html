ABOUT BUSINESS

Our target customers are e-commerce businesses, manufacturers, D2C brands and SMEs that want outsourced warehouse operations with better information visibility.

What WareFlow 360 does

WareFlow 360 provides storage, inventory management, order processing, packing, dispatch coordination and management reporting through a single digital interface.
